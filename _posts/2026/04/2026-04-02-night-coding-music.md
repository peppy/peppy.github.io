I'm usually most productive at night. No matter how I try to rotate my sleep pattern to something resembling normalcy, or coax my body to think and work otherwise.

Here's a few of my heavy rotation albums which really sets the mood:

***

## Night Language - 36

![[Pasted image 20260402065229.jpeg]]

[bandcamp](https://purelifetapes.bandcamp.com/album/night-language) \| [apple music](https://music.apple.com/au/album/night-language/1861144374) \| [youtube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hlhYDJ4Fooc)

A short album featuring atmospheric ambient scapes and lush polyrhythms. The kind you can put on repeat and forget about it for some hours.

## Overwerk - Night Drive 001

![[Pasted image 20260402065735.jpeg]]

[apple music](https://music.apple.com/us/playlist/night-drive-001/pl.24f789acb0d847858e86cd6ade0b5bab) \| [youtube](https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLa4yK1a5RlqIzehtS9M0Sk-_kanPVtEyJ)

A hidden away playlist collection by the artist OVERWERK, featuring their own tracks plus a very well thought out curation of other artists' tracks which form a music journey that I can't get enough of. If the sun is down and I'm feeling like productivity but not sure what to listen to, this is my go-to!

If you enjoy this, go searching for the [others in the series](https://www.overwerk.com/music). I think they're up to 006 now. There's also "Day Drive" and a few other random ones like "Homewerk" which are great.

## PAX JAPONICA GROOVE - knock!Back!Rock!

![[Pasted image 20260402073129.jpeg]]

[apple music](https://music.apple.com/au/album/knock-back-rock/735926720) \| [youtube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UFqSiaydnGA)

Something a bit more upbeat maybe? PAX JAPONICA have a great vibe going, somewhere between rock and house. Their piano backed tracks are great. Not one specifically on this album, but [Picture of Universe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gVWOibNPzQc) is what got me into them as an artist, recommend a listen.

## Kasablanca - Higher Resolution (Side B)

![[Pasted image 20260402074705.jpeg]]

[apple music](https://music.apple.com/au/album/higher-resolution-side-b/1842096366) \| [youtube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3Wqb3m7GYzI)

Okay now we're in the zone. Bring on the dance tracks. This album is a banger. Oh, and there's a "side A" as well which may be a better entry point, but "side B" is my favourite. The use of gapless transitions between tracks is just so well done.

"There's a machine that gets into the head of everyone that hears it.."
